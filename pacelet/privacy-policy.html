Privacy Policy for Pacelet

Last Updated: January 5, 2026

Introduction

Pacelet ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children who use our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information from children under the age of 13, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

By using Pacelet, parents/guardians consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information Collected from Parents

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Full name (to personalize the parent dashboard)

Information Collected from Children (with parental consent)

  • Child's first name (for personalization)
  • Daily step count (via Google Fit API)
  • Screen time usage (via Android Usage Stats API - total time spent in apps)
  • Installed apps list (app names and package names - for parental control features)
  • App usage statistics (which apps child uses and for how long - for screen time monitoring)
  • Device information (Android version, device model for compatibility)
  • Photos (user-submitted for discovery missions - optional feature)

Automatically Collected Information

  • App usage data (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, anonymized)
  • Performance metrics (Firebase Performance, anonymized)

How We Use Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

  1. Provide app functionality: Calculate earned screen time based on physical activity
  2. Parental monitoring: Display child's activity data to parents in dashboard
  3. Parental control: Enable parents to:
    • View installed apps on child's device
    • Monitor app usage time
    • Block/allow specific apps
    • Set daily screen time limits
  4. App improvement: Analyze crash reports and performance (anonymized)
  5. Communication: Send important updates to parents (via email)

We do NOT:

  • Sell or rent children's information to third parties
  • Use children's information for targeted advertising
  • Share children's information except as described in this policy

Third-Party Services

Pacelet uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication: Email/password login (parents only)
  • Firebase Firestore: Store user data (encrypted at rest)
  • Firebase Analytics: Understand app usage (anonymized)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Diagnose crashes (anonymized)
  • Firebase Performance: Monitor app performance (anonymized)

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Fit

  • Purpose: Read daily step count for activity tracking
  • Data Access: Read-only access to step count data

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage and Security

Storage Location

  • Data is stored on Google Cloud servers (Firebase Firestore)
  • Region: asia-south1 (Mumbai, India)
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest

Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
User accounts Until account deletion
Daily activity data 7 days (then aggregated)
Aggregated reports 30 days
Photos (missions) 30 days or upon approval/deletion
Crash reports 90 days

Parental Rights (COPPA Compliance)

Parents have the right to:

  1. Review their child's personal information
  2. Request deletion of their child's information
  3. Refuse further collection of their child's information
  4. Withdraw consent at any time

How to Exercise Your Rights

  • In-App: Go to Child Settings → Manage Data → Delete Child Profile
  • Email: Contact us at vijaytank132@gmail.com with subject "COPPA Data Request"

Response Time: We will respond within 7 business days.

Children's Privacy

We do NOT:

  • Require children to provide more information than necessary
  • Collect precise location data (GPS) from children
  • Use children's data for behavioral advertising
  • Share children's information with third parties for marketing
  • Share installed apps list or usage data with anyone except the parent

We DO:

  • Require verifiable parental consent before collecting child data
  • Provide parents with control over their child's information
  • Use industry-standard security measures
  • Comply with COPPA and applicable privacy laws
